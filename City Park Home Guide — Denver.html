    Good to know

    Altitude

    Denver sits at 5,280 ft. Drink more water than usual and go easy on alcohol the first day.

    Getting around

    RTD bus routes run along Colorado Blvd and 17th Ave; downtown and Union Station are a quick rideshare or bike ride away. Veo e-bikes are usually parked around the park.

    Weather

    Sunny and dry most days, but afternoon thunderstorms and sudden temperature swings are common — layer up.

    Parking

    Street parking is free and generally easy in this neighborhood, though City Park events can fill nearby blocks.
